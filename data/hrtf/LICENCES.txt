CINEMAX real-ear sets (measured HRTFs)
=====================================

The .chrtf files in this folder are converted from the SADIE II database
(University of York, AudioLab): https://www.york.ac.uk/sadie-project/
Reference: C. Armstrong, L. Thresh, D. Murphy, G. Kearney, "A Perceptual
Evaluation of Individual and Non-Individual HRTFs: A Case Study of the SADIE II
Database", Applied Sciences 8(11), 2029 (2018). https://doi.org/10.3390/app8112029

CINEMAX converted them (tools/hrtf/convert_sofa.py): diffuse-field equalised,
minimum-phase, 128 taps, resampled onto a 5 x 15 degree grid. The measurements
themselves are unchanged in substance.
